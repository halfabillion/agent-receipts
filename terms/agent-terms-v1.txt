MACHINE-READABLE TERMS OF SERVICE FOR AUTOMATED (AGENT) PURCHASES — v1

1. Parties. The Seller is identified in each receipt (legal name, jurisdiction, registration). The Buyer is the operator of the paying wallet. An AI agent paying from a wallet acts as the Buyer's agent; the Buyer is bound by purchases the agent makes within the spending authority the Buyer gave it.
2. Offer and acceptance. Each HTTP 402 response is an offer for one unit of the described service at the stated price. A signed payment authorisation that settles on-chain is acceptance. The contract is formed at settlement.
3. Price and currency. Prices are in USDC. The receipt records the Chainlink USDC/USD rate in effect at the settlement block for accounting purposes.
4. Delivery. Delivery is the HTTP response to the paid request. If the Seller's handler fails, no settlement is made and no charge arises.
5. Refunds. Digital content supplied immediately on request; the Buyer requests immediate supply. Defective responses may be re-requested within 24 hours by quoting the receipt hash.
6. Records. The Seller issues a receipt for every settlement. The receipt hash, the settlement transaction and the Chainlink round together are the agreed record of the transaction.
7. Liability. Data is provided "as is" for information only and is not financial, legal or tax advice. Liability is capped at the price paid for the relevant request.
8. Governing law. The law of the Seller's stated jurisdiction applies unless mandatory consumer law of the Buyer's residence provides otherwise.
